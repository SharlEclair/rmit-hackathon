# Rubric

| Criteria | Excellent | Good | Satisfactory | Developing | Limited / Poor | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| **Problem Relevance** | **9–10:** Addresses a clear, genuine and important problem faced by individuals involved in education. Strong understanding of the problem and its context. | **7–8:** Addresses a genuine educational problem with good relevance. | **5–6:** Problem is relevant, but its importance or understanding could be clearer. | **3–4:** Some educational relevance, but the problem is weak, unclear or quite niche. | **0–2:** Problem is unclear, insignificant or not meaningfully related to innovating education. | **10** |
| **Desirability & Impact** | **9–10:** Highly appealing to individuals involved in education with clear potential to meaningfully improve their experience or solve the identified problem. | **7–8:** Strong educational appeal and likely to provide useful benefits. | **5–6:** Some clear value, though adoption or impact may be limited. | **3–4:** Limited evidence that individuals involved in education would want or benefit from the solution. | **0–2:** Little demonstrated value, desirability or potential impact. | **10** |
| **Solution & Creativity** | **7–8:** Highly creative or innovative approach that solves the problem effectively. Demonstrates strong thought beyond obvious solutions. | **5–6:** Good solution with some originality or creative features. | **3–4:** Sensible solution but relatively conventional or lacking differentiation. | **2:** Limited creativity or solution does not fully address the problem. | **0–1:** Solution is unclear, unsuitable or shows little original thought. | **8** |
| **Design (UI/UX)** | **7–8:** Highly intuitive, visually clear and easy to navigate. | **Presentation** | **5:** Exceptionally clear and engaging explanation of the problem, solution, demo and impact. | **4:** Clear and effective presentation covering the important elements. | **3:** Understandable presentation but some elements lack clarity or polish. | **2:** Difficult to follow or missing important information. | **0–1:** Very unclear, substantially incomplete or does not effectively communicate the project. | **5** |

 Winners will be selected by the judging panel on **Sunday, 4th October**, after live presentations from teams.

 ## Project & Submission Requirements

 - Submit by the deadline, and reach out **ASAP** for any submission issues. Late exceptions cannot be guaranteed.
- Only work within your team. **No external assistance from other individuals is allowed.**
- Only work within the hackathon timeline. **No work from old projects or schoolwork can be submitted.**
- Commit often to your **GitHub repository** to show proof of your progress and development.
- Use of **generative AI** must be appropriately referenced. Please include an explanation of **how and why you used AI** to assist you in your Devpost submission **and presentation**.
